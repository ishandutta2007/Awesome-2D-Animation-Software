## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Carta](https://carta.com/)**

  The dominant equity management platform with **fund administration and LP portfolio management**. Provides cap table management, 409A valuations, fund administration, and investor portfolio tracking for LPs across venture funds.



- **[Juniper Square](https://www.junipersquare.com/)**

  Investment management platform purpose-built for **private markets fund managers and LPs**. Features capital account tracking, LP portal, distribution waterfall calculations, and investor reporting.



- **[Allvue Systems](https://www.allvuesystems.com/)**

  Investment management software for private equity, venture capital, and credit funds. Features fund accounting, LP reporting, portfolio monitoring, and compliance.



- **[Vessel](https://www.vessel.com/)**

  LP portfolio management platform for family offices and institutional investors. Features fund tracking, capital call management, and performance aggregation across private market investments.



- **[Dynamo Software](https://www.dynamosoftware.com/)**

  Alternative investment software for LPs, GPs, and fund administrators. Features portfolio tracking, CRM, fund accounting, and LP reporting.



- **[FundCount](https://www.fundcount.com/)**

  Partnership accounting and investment portfolio management software. Features fund accounting, LP allocations, and performance reporting.



- **[eFront (BlackRock)](https://www.efront.com/)**

  Alternative investment software for LPs, GPs, and fund administrators. Features portfolio management, fund accounting, and LP reporting.



- **[Flow](https://www.flow.com/)**

  LP portfolio management platform. Features fund tracking, capital account management, and performance analytics.



- **[iLevel (S&P Global)](https://www.spglobal.com/)**

  Portfolio monitoring and data collection platform for private markets. Features LP portfolio tracking, performance analytics, and reporting.



- **[Burgiss](https://www.burgiss.com/)**

  Private capital data and analytics for LPs and GPs. Features performance benchmarking, portfolio analytics, and fund data.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Hemrock Fund Accounting & LP Reporting](https://www.hemrock.com/fund-accounting)**

  **The most comprehensive open-source platform for fund accounting and LP reporting** (Apache 2.0, built by a fund CFO). **Paste in bank transactions, run a monthly close, create financial statements and a schedule of investments.** The accounting module brings **plain text accounting to funds**—journal entries stored as plain text files, making import/export easy and AI-friendly . **LP reporting, aggregated**: paste in the schedule of investments from your fund admin, SPV admin, or spreadsheet, and the app creates **structured capital account statements and aggregated performance metrics across entities** . Optional **limited partner portal** for delivering aggregated reporting. **Your own MCP server**: launch your own MCP server for access to your data via Claude or ChatGPT, running on your own infrastructure with authentication . **Self-host free, no per-seat fees** .



- **[Hemrock Portfolio Reporting](https://www.hemrock.com/reporting)**

  **Portfolio monitoring, deal flow, LP reporting, and fund accounting in one platform** (Apache 2.0, open-source) . **Start with Portfolio Reporting**: forward founder updates (PDF, Excel, PowerPoint, plain text) to an inbound address; AI identifies the company, extracts metrics, and flags anything uncertain for review . MRR, burn, headcount, and custom KPIs chart over time. **LP Reporting**: paste a statement and AI maps columns into a dated position, building a capital record over time; pull a capital account as of any date. Every LP rolls up across vehicles. LPs read results in a **private, fund-branded portal** where an AI analyst answers questions using only that LP's own materials . **Fund Operations**: double-entry ledger per vehicle (fund, SPV, direct deal, GP entity); import bank feeds, book calls and distributions, close a period to allocate income into each partner's capital account . **Use the components you need**—each product is optional .



- **[Post-Investment Platform](https://github.com/Oliveluo666/post-investment-platform)**

  **Open-source post-investment management workbench for VC/PE teams** (open-source) . Features **portfolio overview dashboard**, **project details** (overview, financial data, timeline), **Excel batch import** with multi-sheet templates covering equity, financials, and board data, **financial dashboard** by year and period, **compliance todos** with custom types, **DOCX report export**, and **Excel template download** . Built with React 18, Vite, Ant Design, Tailwind CSS, and PostgreSQL (CloudBase). **Demo mode available**—runs with built-in sample data in 2 minutes without backend configuration . **The most accessible open-source post-investment workbench for VC/PE teams.**



- **[Malak](https://github.com/ayinke-llc/malak)**

  **Open-source alternative to Carta, Pulley, and Shareworks for investor relations** (AGPL-3.0) . Features **rich monthly investor updates** with multimedia content and document attachments, **KPI tracking** in customizable dashboards, **fundraising pipeline CRM**, **data rooms** for due diligence, and **real-time stakeholder feedback** . Custom domain support, user management, integrations, and audit logs. **61 GitHub stars**, v0.9.13 . **The most focused open-source platform for LP communication and fundraising pipeline management.**



- **[Rotki](https://github.com/rotki)**

  **Open-source portfolio tracking, analytics, accounting, and management application** (AGPL-3.0) . **4,035 GitHub stars**. Protects privacy—all data stays local. Features **multi-asset tracking** (stocks, ETFs, crypto, bonds, options, real estate, NFTs), **FIFO cost basis**, **dividends and fund look-through**, **cash flow forecasting**, **tax-category rules**, and **blockchain imports** across Ethereum, Arbitrum, Avalanche, Bitcoin, Solana, Tron, and Hyperliquid . **The most comprehensive open-source portfolio tracker for LPs with diverse asset classes.**



- **[OpenBB](https://github.com/OpenBB-finance/OpenBB)**

  **AI-powered research analysis platform for investment firms** (open-source) . **65,718 GitHub stars**. Features **secure local or private cloud deployment** (SOC2 II certified), **data integration** (proprietary, licensed, and public data), **custom AI agents**, and **flexible customizable UI** for team collaboration . Used by investment firms managing billions in assets including Angeles Investments . **The most comprehensive open-source research platform for institutional investors.**



- **[Fincept Terminal](https://github.com/Fincept-Corporation/FinceptTerminal)**

  **Open-source financial platform with CFA-level analytics, AI agents, and 100+ data connectors** (AGPL-3.0) . Features **CFA Level 1/2/3 curriculum in Python**, **DCF models**, **portfolio optimization**, **risk metrics** (VaR, Sharpe), **20+ investor personas** (Buffett, Dalio, Graham), **hedge fund strategies** (Bridgewater, Citadel, Renaissance), **100+ data connectors** (DBnomics, Polygon, Kraken, PostgreSQL, Kafka), and **3D maritime tracking** with geopolitical frameworks . **The most feature-dense open-source terminal for institutional portfolio analysis.**



- **[Macrofolio](https://github.com/nutraz/macrofolio)**

  **Open-source multi-asset portfolio tracker** (open-source) . Features **stocks, ETFs, crypto, gold, real estate, fixed income, NFTs** tracking, **self-custodial** with zero centralized storage of sensitive data, **on-chain verification** via Polygon & Base, **real-time updates**, and **premium risk/diversification analysis** (country/sector exposure) . **The most accessible open-source multi-asset tracker for LPs tracking diverse portfolios.**



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **[Oikos](https://github.com/atsob/Oikos)** — Self-hosted personal finance app tracking real investment portfolios with multi-currency stocks/ETFs/bonds/crypto, FIFO cost basis, dividends, fund look-through, cash flow forecasting, and tax-category rules. FastAPI + Postgres + React, AGPL-3.0 .

- **[JAL (Just Another Ledger)](https://pypi.org/project/jal/2026.9.3/)** — Personal finance tracking with multiple accounts, any currencies, stocks/ETFs/options/bonds/futures, crypto wallets on Ethereum/Arbitrum/Avalanche/Bitcoin/Solana/Tron, tax reports for Russia and Portugal, and broker/bank/exchange statement imports . **The most feature-complete open-source personal ledger for tracking diverse investments.**

- **[OnePortfolio](https://explore.market.dev/ecosystems/python/projects/oneportfolio)** — Flask web app for organizing investments by named portfolios (stocks, gold, ETFs, crypto, retirement) with cash events, transactions, dividends, Average Cost Method, realized P&L, and multi-user accounts with strict per-user data isolation .

- **[LP Capital Lifecycle Modeler](https://github.com/topics/hedge-alternative)** — Open-source LP capital lifecycle and waterfall modeler with call pacing, preferred accrual, peak funding need, and J-curve dynamics. Validated against an independent second model .

- **[Fund Document Intelligence](https://github.com/topics/hedge-alternative)** — Open-source schema-validated extraction from LPAs, PPMs, capital account statements, and side letters via PDF vision .

- **[Distribution Notice Generator](https://skills.rest/skill/distribution-notice-generator)** — Automates LP distribution notices and tax previews with waterfall logic (pref, return of capital, GP promote), per-LP capital accounts, batch CSVs for wires, and template notices .

- Community **n8n** workflows for LP reporting automation and capital call alert routing.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **Hemrock** for fund accounting and LP reporting with **Post-Investment Platform** for VC/PE portfolio monitoring, **Malak** for investor communication and data rooms, **Rotki** or **Oikos** for multi-asset tracking, and **PostgreSQL** for persistence—creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade LP portfolio management platform.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- LP portfolio management systems handling financial data must comply with applicable securities regulations, fund administration standards, and data protection requirements. **Legal review and qualified fund administration expertise remain essential.**

- **Open-source LP portfolio management is maturing but specialized.** Hemrock provides the most comprehensive open-source fund accounting and LP reporting platform with plain-text accounting, aggregated capital account statements, and MCP server integration . Malak provides investor communication and fundraising pipeline management . Post-Investment Platform provides VC/PE portfolio monitoring . **No single open-source tool provides the end-to-end LP portfolio management of Juniper Square or Allvue.** Integration work is required.

- **Plain-text accounting is the open-source advantage.** Hemrock's journal entries stored as plain text files make import/export easy and AI-friendly . This provides auditability and portability that proprietary systems cannot match.

- **MCP server integration enables AI-native LP reporting.** Hemrock's MCP server allows launching your own server for access to your data via Claude or ChatGPT, running on your own infrastructure with authentication . This is the emerging pattern for AI-native fund operations.

- **LP reporting aggregation across entities is a key capability.** Hemrock aggregates capital account statements and performance metrics across funds, SPVs, and spreadsheets—the common reality for LPs with investments across multiple vehicles .

- Self-hosted open-source solutions require proper security hardening, financial data protection, and ongoing maintenance. Fund data is highly sensitive—access controls and audit trails are essential.
